# Voiceover script — e2a launch v5 (identity story)

## Intro (Frame 1)
    Meet your new agent. Ready to work.

## Hook (Frame 2)
    You've got customers, orders, meetings, vendors. You want your agent to handle it all.

## No identity (Frame 3)
    It can think, reason, even draft the reply. But it can't reach anyone. It has no address.

## Reveal (Frame 4)
    So give it an address. Its own identity.

## It works (Frame 5)
    Now it gets the work done. Answering customers, booking meetings, chasing orders, routing leads. Every line, over email.

## Trust (Frame 6)
    Your agent acts. You stay in control. Big sends wait for your ok.

## Close (Frame 7)
    Your agent doesn't need another dashboard. It needs an address.

## Brand (Frame 8)
    Give your agent an address. e2a.
